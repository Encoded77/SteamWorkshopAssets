[h1]Rebalance Patches[/h1]
Opinionated balance tweaks and cross-mod compatibility patches, plus a substantial amount of custom content to expand existing mods and fill gaps where needed, all aimed at getting a big pile of mods to play nicely together as a cohesive experience.

{{image:d10-banner-details}}

This mod touches a lot of things, and it keeps growing as more mods get patched together. If a mod misbehaves, please try to reproduce the issue with this patch disabled before reporting it to the respective mod author.

{{image:d20-banner-overhauls}}

{{image:d25-card-cybernetics}}

Integrated: Core, Royalty, Biotech, EPOE-Forked, Integrated Implants, GiTS Cyberbrains, Psychic Implants, Altered Carbon and Vanilla Races Expanded - Android, with module hosting through EBSG Framework and thoracic frames that take Big and Small's mechanical ribs where present.

{{image:d26-card-psycast}}

Integrated: Royalty, Vanilla Psycasts Expanded, Eltex Weaponry, Vali's Charged Arsenal - Eltex Series and Vanilla Factions Expanded - Deserters, which carries the techprint.

{{image:d21-card-genetics-research}}

Integrated: ReSplice: Core, Gene Extractor Tiers, Gene Nodes - Genes for Sale, Gene Fabrication, Gene Ripper, and Vanilla Quests Expanded - Ancients, where a new archogen engineering capstone lets you build the archogen injector and its lab facilities yourself at archite-tier costs. Ancient vaults still work as the early route.

{{image:d22-card-gene-dedup}}

The gene list gets a lot shorter. Alpha Genes keeps its immunities and armor, Big and Small its body mechanics, the specialist Vanilla Races Expanded packs their specialty, Det's Xenotypes their quirks; Alpha Genes' animal summons go entirely, and VRE - Hussar's per-weapon aptitudes are replaced by four. WVC's strongest races have to be earned through its own events and morphs instead of walking in with a raid.

{{image:d31-card-xenotype-factions}}

Settled outlanders lean industrial, rough outlanders frontier, pirates predatory, the Empire aristocratic; deep-space xenotypes concentrate in Rimsenal's Spacer factions and Odyssey's Salvagers. Covers Det's Xenotypes, the Rimsenal xenotype packs, Vanilla Races Expanded, Highborn Xenotype, WVC - Xenotypes and Genes and Alpha Genes. Every reworked roster keeps at least a third of baseliners.

{{image:d23-card-scifi-renaming}}

Covers Big and Small - Races, Heaven and Hell, Genes & More, Yokai, Lamias, Slimes, More Xenotypes, WVC and Alpha Genes. Jotun become gigants, dvergr deepkin, svartalfs umbrakin, trolls regenerants; angels become ascendants and demons abyssals. Muspelheim becomes the Cinderhold Dominion, Niflheim the Permafrost Clans.

{{image:d24-card-expertise}}

Adds mechanitor expertises (Mechlord, Mechwright) and psycast expertises (Overchanneler, Quietist). Expertises from Alpha Skills, Hauts' Framework, Vanilla Fishing Expanded and Vanilla Gravship Expanded are folded in, and stats from Integrated Implants, Mechanoid Upgrades, Altered Carbon and Vanilla Psycasts Expanded ride along when those are loaded. Pawns keep any expertise they already picked.

{{image:d30-banner-patches}}

[list]
[*]RimIOT - Logistic Matrix: cheaper builds, basic components only, no power consumption.
[*]Altered Carbon: disables the redundant ranged shield belt from VAE Accessories in favour of the harder to get cuirassier belt, a slider for the casting relay's needlecasting range, and a sleeve broker orbital trader that sells empty sleeve bodies, neural stacks and genepacks so the transhuman economy has a buy-side.
[*]Altered Carbon scenario, "Digitized": a solo naked-brutality start where your digitized mind wakes as an empty sleeve needlecast from a derelict matrix sealed in a bunker. You start with nothing; destroy the matrix and the sleeve dies with it.
[*]GiTS Cyberbrains: only basic cyberbrains on the market, surgeries via EPOE-Forked brain surgery, post-basic cyberization at ultratech tier, harsher extreme mental break, tidied-up research tree.
[*]Progression Robotics: streamlines the per-mech and per-gear unlock research into a handful of shared, themed projects (standard combat mechs, utility mechs, bandwidth gear, mechlord equipment and more) instead of one long flat list.
[*]Odyssey: the long-range passenger shuttle gets 2000 chemfuel and 2000 kg cargo capacity.
[*]Alpha Genes: genes join vanilla genepacks, alphapacks/mixedpacks removed.
[*]Integrated Implants: slime-safe shoulder turrets, Alpha Mechs chip requirements for mechanitor implant research, levitators ignore water, signal boosters stack with AG command range genes.
[*]Rimsenal: armors and modular weapons gated behind the corp techs. Spacer trade caravans lose their mechanoid guards.
[*]Research prereq cleanups: VFE Pirates warcasket weapons, Impact Weaponry, Spacer Arsenal. Eltex weapons only on psycasters.
[*]Ushanka's Biological Warfare: streamlined research tree.
[*]Ideology: inspirations respect precepts, warlike factions can't roll pacifist vows, and two new opposed genetics memes: Genecrafted and Bloodline Purity, that react to how far a colonist's genes stray from their xenotype template.
[*]Vanilla Achievements Expanded: no more summoning traders on demand, the powerful point exchanges cost more, and cheaper ones are added.
[*]Vanilla & DLC: mixed modded mechanoid raid groups, creep joiners accept all human surgeries, gene complexity sliders, a hand-picked gene list for the VQEA archogen injector, vacuum resistance nerfs on modded armors.
[/list]

This list is not exhaustive and there is more patches for a lot of mods available in the settings.

{{image:d40-banner-compatibility}}

Patches only apply when the mods they target are loaded, so nothing here is a hard dependency. Every tweak has its own toggle in the mod settings (restart required), and toggles whose requirements aren't met are hidden.

{{image:d45-shot-settings}}

Full details per toggle in the mod settings and in the documentation on GitHub. The mod's own text is set up for translation if someone want to contribute !.
